Gift Aid

Add 25% to your giving, at no cost to you

If you pay UK tax, Gift Aid lets us reclaim 25p for every £1 you give — so £10 becomes £12.50. You only need to do this once; it covers your future giving and the past four years.

We store your declaration securely and use it only to claim Gift Aid on your giving. HMRC requires us to keep it for as long as you give, and for six years afterwards.
