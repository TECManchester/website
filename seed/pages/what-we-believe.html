Statement of faith

What we believe

We're a Pentecostal church — Bible-centred and Spirit-filled. Here's what that actually means, in plain English.

One God, three persons

There is one God, manifested in three persons — Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the Son of God, and the only way to the Father.

Matthew 1:18–25; John 14:6

The Bible

The Bible is God's inspired Word.

2 Timothy 3:16

Salvation

All people need salvation, and it is received freely by grace through faith — believing in your heart and confessing with your mouth.

Romans 3:23; Romans 10:9; Ephesians 2:8

Death, resurrection and return

Jesus died, rose again and is coming again; the dead will rise.

1 Corinthians 15:4; Acts 1:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17

Baptism and the Lord's Supper

We practise Water Baptism and the Lord's Supper.

Matthew 28:19; Matthew 26:26–29

The Holy Spirit and healing

We believe in the Baptism of the Holy Spirit, and that healing is provided in the atonement of Christ.

Mark 16:17–18; Acts 1:8; James 5:14–15; 1 Peter 2:24
